When Miles asks for tools that auto-promote Fabricate on third-party platforms (Reddit bots, auto-reply systems, comment automation), push back and redirect to a monitoring tool instead — surface threads to his inbox, let him reply personally.

**Why:** 2026-05-12 he asked for a Reddit bot that auto-inserts Fabricate wherever 3D printing comes up. Refused on three grounds: Reddit ToS (account/domain ban risk), community backlash (r/3Dprinting et al will scorched-earth a brand caught spamming), and deceptive to the person asking. He accepted the redirect immediately and we built the Reddit monitor instead — see [[project-reddit-monitor]]. Validated approach, not a contested correction.

**How to apply:**
- Don't build auto-posters, auto-replyers, or "engagement automation" for organic-feel platforms (Reddit, Discord, niche forums).
- Marketing automation on owned channels (email lists, in-app, transactional notifications) is fine.
- When unsure, propose the monitoring + human-reply pattern as the alternative.
