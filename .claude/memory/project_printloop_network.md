**Target printer archetypes (pre-load slicing profiles for these):**
- High-Speed Pro: Bambu Lab P2S / X1C, Prusa Core One, Creality K1C (London startup favorites)
- Reliable Hobby: Prusa MK4S, Elegoo Neptune 4 Pro (university lab staples)
- Large Format: Original Prusa XL, Creality Ender-5 Max (architectural prototype focus)

**University outreach partners (the "University Hook"):**
Imperial College Hackspace, UCL Making Institute, Queen Mary London. Students onboarded as Makers provide distributed hyper-local coverage for East and West London design hubs.

**Why:** The marketplace only works with supply density. University hackspaces are the cheapest acquisition channel for Makers in London.

**How to apply:** When building the Maker onboarding flow or slicing-profile manager, these printer models are the default list. Admin UI should surface network density by borough, not just total active Makers.
