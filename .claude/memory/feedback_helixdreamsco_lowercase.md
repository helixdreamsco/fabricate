The parent brand name is **helixdreamsco** — always lowercase. Applies to public business name, support copy, maker-facing reassurance lines, dashboard fields we control, and code strings we author (e.g. statement descriptor input).

**Why:** Miles's brand convention. The legal entity is registered as HELIXDREAMSCO LTD (Companies House style); the styled brand is lowercase.

**How to apply:**
- When writing UI copy, dashboard values, or code constants that contain the brand, lowercase it.
- Bank statements may force-uppercase Stripe descriptors regardless — that's outside our control and not a violation of the rule.
- The legal entity string `HELIXDREAMSCO LTD` is the registered Companies House form and should be left as-is in legal documents that quote the registry; otherwise, lowercase.
